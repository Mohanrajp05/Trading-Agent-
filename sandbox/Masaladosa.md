# Masala Dosa Recipe

Source: [Swasthi's Recipes – Masala Dosa Recipe (Restaurant Style)](https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/masala-dosa-recipe/)

## Why this one
- Highly rated and popular.
- Classic restaurant-style masala dosa.
- Clear separation of batter, potato masala, and cooking steps.

## Ingredients

### Dosa batter
- Idli rice / parboiled rice
- Urad dal
- Chana dal
- Thick poha
- Fenugreek seeds
- Salt
- Water

### Potato masala
- Potatoes
- Oil or ghee
- Mustard seeds
- Cumin seeds
- Urad dal
- Chana dal
- Curry leaves
- Green chilies
- Onion
- Ginger
- Turmeric
- Salt
- Coriander leaves
- Lemon juice

## Method
1. Soak the rice and dals, then grind into a smooth batter.
2. Ferment the batter until airy and lightly sour.
3. Boil potatoes and prepare the spiced potato filling.
4. Heat a dosa tawa, pour batter, and spread thin in circles.
5. Add oil around the edges and cook until crisp and golden.
6. Place potato masala on one side, fold, and serve hot.

## Tips
- Ferment the batter well for crisp dosa.
- Use a hot tawa for even spreading.
- Don’t overfill the dosa.
- Serve with coconut chutney and sambar.

## Summary
A great masala dosa is all about a well-fermented rice-lentil batter, a flavorful potato masala, and a very hot pan for a thin, crisp finish.
