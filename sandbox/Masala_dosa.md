# Masala Dosa Recipe

## Introduction
Masala Dosa is a popular South Indian breakfast consisting of a crispy crepe made from fermented rice and lentil batter, filled with flavorful spiced potato masala. It's often served with coconut chutney and sambar.

## Ingredients
### For Masala Dosa Batter
- ½ cup urad dal (skinned black gram)
- 3 tablespoons chana dal (bengal gram)
- 1½ cups raw sona masuri rice
- ½ cup idli rice (optional, can skip or substitute with raw rice)
- 3 tablespoons poha (flattened rice)
- ½ teaspoon methi seeds (fenugreek seeds)
- ¾ teaspoon salt (or as needed)
- 1¼ cups water (for blending dal)
- ½ to ¾ cups water (for blending rice)

### For Potato Masala
- 2 cups potatoes, cubed
- 1 tablespoon oil
- ¼ to ½ teaspoon mustard seeds
- ½ teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1 teaspoon chana dal (optional)
- 1 teaspoon urad dal (optional)
- 1 pinch asafoetida (hing) (optional)
- 1 teaspoon grated ginger
- ¾ to 1 cup thinly sliced onions
- 1 sprig curry leaves
- 2 green chilies, chopped
- ¼ teaspoon turmeric powder
- ½ to ¾ teaspoon salt (adjust to taste)
- 2 tablespoons coriander leaves, finely chopped
- Butter (as needed for cooking)

## Instructions
### Step 1: Prepare the Batter
1. **Soak Ingredients:**  
   Add urad dal, chana dal, and methi seeds to a bowl. Soak them for 4 to 5 hours. Soak poha for 30 minutes before blending.
2. **Blend Dal:**  
   Drain the dal and blend it with poha and some water until frothy. Transfer to a bowl.
3. **Blend Rice:**  
   Drain the rice and blend it with water to a coarse consistency. Mix with the dal batter.
4. **Fermentation:**  
   Cover and ferment the batter in a warm place for 6 to 16 hours.

### Step 2: Make Potato Masala
1. **Cook Potatoes:**  
   Steam or boil potatoes until cooked but not mushy. Cool and crumble them.
2. **Sauté Spices:**  
   Heat oil, add mustard seeds, cumin, and chana dal. Once golden, add hing, ginger, onions, chilies, and curry leaves. Cook until onions are golden.
3. **Combine:**  
   Add crumbled potatoes, turmeric, and salt. Mix well and set aside.

### Step 3: Make Masala Dosa
1. **Heat Pan:**  
   Heat a tawa (griddle) and grease with oil or butter.
2. **Spread Batter:**  
   Pour a ladle of batter on the hot pan and spread it in a circular motion.
3. **Cook:**  
   Drizzle oil around the edges, cook until golden brown, flip, and cook the other side.
4. **Add Filling:**  
   Place potato masala on one half and fold the dosa.

### Step 4: Serve
Serve hot with coconut chutney and sambar.

## Notes
- The consistency of the batter is key; it should be thick yet pourable.
- Experiment with the addition of chiroti rava or semolina for extra crispness.

### Tips
- Ensure your pan is the right temperature to achieve crisp dosas.
- Use a cast iron pan for the best results in color and texture.

Enjoy your homemade Masala Dosa!
